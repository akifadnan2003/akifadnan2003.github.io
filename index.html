About

What I work on

I'm an AI Engineer working on voice agents, LLM-based automation and data pipelines on Google Cloud.

Recently I built a real-time voice agent on Twilio, Deepgram and Gemini. The conversation flow is handled by deterministic code, the LLM only writes the wording, and every reply passes an output check before it is spoken. I have also built a shipment tracking platform with FastAPI, Next.js and BigQuery, a rule-based risk scoring service, and a multilingual ticket classification service.

I care most about the parts that make AI systems dependable: latency, testing against failure, and keeping personal data away from models.
